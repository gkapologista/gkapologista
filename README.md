# Hi there, I'm GK 👋

## 🚀 About Me

**Senior Frontend Developer & Full Stack Engineer**

I architect, build, and ship production-grade web and mobile applications—with over **5 years** of experience turning complex requirements into responsive, high-performance products.

My core strength is front-end architecture with **React.js**, **TypeScript**, and **Redux**, complemented by full-stack breadth across **Node.js**, **Python/Django**, and **PHP/Laravel**, plus cross-platform mobile development with **React Native**. I prioritize clean, type-safe code, system performance, and user-centric design to build products that are technically sound and maintainable at scale.

Cum Laude CS graduate and former University Instructor—I leverage strong theoretical foundations and hands-on engineering to solve complex architectural challenges with depth and practical efficiency.
